Renaming a Codable property renamed its JSON key and destroyed the user's Notes

## Where the overhaul is

The scratchpad is Notes: a card carrying the title and a Craft launch button,
with a vertical tab rail joined to the note by one unioned Path
(NoteJoinedShape) so the selected tab reads as the note's front edge. ccp-ydo.1
through .5 all closed and screenshot-verified. ccp-32f closed too — the panel
could never cut/copy/paste because an `.accessory` app with no `NSApp.mainMenu`
has nothing to dispatch the standard key equivalents.

Not done, and it is the user's own spec: **ccp-13f**, the other widgets dimming
to 69% a few seconds into typing. Build it as a panel-level focus capability a
widget declares, not a flag threaded into every card — and build its idle clock
so **ccp-ydo.6** (Apple Intelligence auto-titling a new note after 6s quiet) can
subscribe to it. Three independent timers over one text field is the shape to
avoid; that is why those two beads reference each other.

## The mistake, because the shape of it will recur

I renamed `NotesDocument.pads` to `.notes`. Synthesised CodingKeys meant the
stored key moved with the property, every existing document stopped decoding,
and `loadApplyingRetention` resolved the decode failure *by persisting a fresh
empty document over the user's bytes* — before a keystroke. Three tabs gone; no
Time Machine destination and no APFS snapshots on this machine.

The rename was the bug. The write-on-decode-failure was why it could not be
undone, and that is the half worth carrying forward: two `bd remember` traps
(a-codable-rename-is-a-data-migration,
never-resolve-a-decode-failure-by-writing) and a CLAUDE.md convention.

Worth knowing: the correctness reviewer then found my *fix* was also wrong —
the no-write path only held until the panel closed, because flushSave still
persisted the stand-in. Both rounds are in the diff. I would not have caught
that one alone, which is an argument for running the review pass on data-path
changes even when the change is small and you just wrote the test for it.

## Mid-thought

The rail is capped at 84pt inside a 300pt lane, and the note is the smaller half
of a small card. It reads fine at one note; I do not think it reads well at
eight. The honest fix is ccp-lh1.2 (two-axis sizing) rather than a wider literal,
and ccp-ydo.2's close reason says so — but nobody has looked at Notes with a
realistic number of tabs yet. Do that before adding anything to the rail.

Also unresolved and deliberately left: nothing reads `scratchpadDocument` from
upstream's `com.vorssaint.utils` domain, so the floating pad and the Notes widget
have drifted into two separate documents on this machine despite sharing a key
name. The adapter comment claims they are one. Worth a bead if it bites.
