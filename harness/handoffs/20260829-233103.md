The load-bearing call, made by laurel under grilling: an unknown widget id is
never destroyed. It decodes verbatim, survives a save from a build that cannot
render it, and resolves to a visible `.unavailable` slot rather than vanishing.
That is why decode is dumb and `normalized()` is a separate step you call — if
you find yourself tempted to move normalization into `init(from:)`, that is the
invariant you would be breaking.
