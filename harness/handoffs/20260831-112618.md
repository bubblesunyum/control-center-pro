What I'd want the next session to know:

The bridge rule ("a shim may restate a type, but may not decide anything about
one") has now survived one round of pressure and bent once already — the
AsyncStream conversion went in under it and came back out as ccp-bn9. It held
because a reviewer named it, not because the rule caught it. Watch the second
shim, whenever a widget needs one: the rule's real test is whether someone
writing at speed notices the difference between restating and deciding.

audioCapture hardcoded to .unknown. Both are forced by macOS exposing no read,
so there is no adapter-side version that knows more. But they are decisions in
a file that claims to hold none, and if a third one appears the rule is
probably wrong rather than the code.
