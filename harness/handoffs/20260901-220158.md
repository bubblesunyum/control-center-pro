# handoff 2026-09-01

Raised the deployment floor 14.4 → 26 (ccp-ty1) so psymail-mini can be carried as
a widget. The raise is not free and that is the part worth carrying forward: it
silently changed timer behaviour, and only one test noticed.

Was mid-thought on whether `tolerance: .zero` is the right shape at all, or
whether a small `repeatingTick(every:)` capability belongs in CCPUI so no future
sampler has to remember the tolerance argument. Three call-sites is exactly the
threshold CLAUDE.md says to stop special-casing at, and I stopped one short of
acting on it. If a fourth sampler appears, build the primitive instead.

The rejected approach still tempting me: extracting a ~3.3k-line "engine slice"
of psymail rather than raising the floor. It looked cheaper and it isn't —
psymail's Services/Models/MailStore are already clean of 26-only API, so the
slice bought nothing the floor raise didn't, and it would have left two
divergent copies of the mail engine to keep in step.

Two things that surprised me, both in `bd recall`:
- `Task.sleep(for:)` coalesces under a 26 target. Reads as flake, isn't —
  3/3 fail at 26.0, 3/3 pass at 14.4.
- PlistBuddy's two silent failure modes in scripts/app.sh. Both found by the
  review pass, neither by the gate. Both were in shell, not Swift.

Also unresolved: the wiggle desync fix is unverified *visually*. A screenshot
can't show phase offset, and I didn't launch the app and watch edit mode. If
someone is in there anyway, look at it — the fix is reasoned, not observed.

Next: ccp-8lh (wire PsymailKit as a dependency, MailAdapter + MailWidget, 300pt
lane card — the credential half is already done, see the bead's note). The floor
raise also unblocks ccp-m53, the native reorderable() migration that was parked
waiting on exactly this.
