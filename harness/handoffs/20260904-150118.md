The thing I keep going back and forth on: last session's note said to try
NSParagraphStyle textLists if markers kept causing trouble in .9, and they did
— backspacing into a bullet baked it into the user's text. I went the other way
instead and made the counted-text approach *bigger*, drawing the indent as
spaces too. It is tested and it works, but there are now two decorations in the
text flow the caret can sit inside, and every new rule about them is another
counting rule. If a third one shows up, that is the signal — stop patching and
find out whether SwiftUI's TextEditor honours a paragraph style. I still
haven't spent the twenty minutes to check.

The format bar has never been seen either. It appears on focus now rather than
on selection, which is right for a block command, but that means it holds a
permanent row of a 300pt card the entire time you are typing. On paper it
fits (~170pt). In practice I have no idea, and it is the first thing to look at
once .4 lands.
