# handoff 2026-09-01

The mail widget was never a port that lost the tab bar — it was built on a wrong
premise both repos had written down as intentional. PsymailKit compiled every
view but marked them internal, so ccp-8lh hand-drew a list over PsymailInbox's
data values. psymail now exports the screen (psy-0ke) and ccp-452 hosts it.

What I'd pick up next: nothing is mid-thought, but two things nag. The `.screen`
size is a real capability now and only mail uses it — the second user of it will
tell us whether `width` belongs on `WidgetSize` or on the descriptor, and I'd
rather find that out before a third. And ccp-lst (pin the psymail dependency to
a URL) got *further* away today, not closer: the surface changed shape and the
two repos are more coupled than before. Leave the path dep until the embed has
had real use.

The approach I rejected and still half-want: making `MenuBarRoot` take an
`isEmbedded` flag instead of moving the frame and the resize edge out to the app
lane. It would have been three lines instead of a restructure, and it is the
thing CLAUDE.md warns about in as many words. The restructure is right — the
screen genuinely has no business knowing its own size — but the flag will look
tempting again the next time psymail needs to behave differently under a host,
and it should be refused again.

Two reviewer findings this session were false positives against superseded
screenshots (see `review-sh-ships-stale-captures`, ccp-rh9). Measuring the pixels
settled both in one command each. Worth doing that before acting on any visual
finding.

ccp-caq holds the one design finding I left: psymail paints an opaque background
over CCP's glass. Laurel says it looks fine in situ, so it is parked, not lost.
