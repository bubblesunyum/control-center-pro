The thing I'd want the next session to know: the panel's window is transparent
and every card blurs the desktop for itself, so there is no container view at
all. Two consequences that aren't obvious from the code. First, a click in the
gutter between cards hits the window and goes nowhere — ccp-dz0, and it wants
deciding *with* ccp-lr7.4 rather than after, because "dismiss on outside click"
and "let a gutter click through" are the same hit-testing question asked twice.
Second, window-level `hasShadow` is off: macOS derives a shadow from content
alpha and on separate cards that came out as black blobs in the gaps. If a
future change puts a backdrop view back in, that flag has to come back with it.

I was mid-thought on whether `Layout.laneWidth` being a constant survives contact
with ccp-lr7.6. The panel currently sizes itself from the hosting view's
`fittingSize`, which is honest and removed a pile of arithmetic — but "width grows
leftward up to full screen width" implies a cap the content doesn't know about,
and I suspect the lane count will need clamping against `visibleFrame` somewhere
that isn't the SwiftUI layout. Didn't chase it; nothing is wrong yet.

Rejected and still slightly tempting: hand-computing the panel size from the
declared `WidgetSize` heights. It's tempting because it makes the window size
knowable before layout, which the sub-100ms open would like. It's wrong because
the arithmetic has to agree with what SwiftUI actually did, and the first
disagreement is a card overdrawing its neighbour — which is exactly the bug the
screenshots caught before `fittingSize` replaced it.

Screenshots are worth running (`scripts/capture.sh`). Both real defects this
session were invisible in the diff and obvious in a capture. See the two new
memory keys before debugging either the blur or the capture.
